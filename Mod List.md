# Story Line
- Inter Stellar travel
- Kerbol Solar System Has "SOME" problem
- Eventually, planets will vanish!

- We need to move forward into the space
- When we found there is danger, we will try to move outside
- We will find extra planets around our solar system that we were not aware of
- We do **_everything we can_** in our solar system to survive
- Eventually, we have to leave
- We look outside, we find a new solar system
- Time to leave home!!!!

When they get to the new solar system, the first chapter ends.
